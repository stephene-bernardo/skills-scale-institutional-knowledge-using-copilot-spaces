# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project management approach focused on clear outcomes, small increments, and shared ownership. The lifecycle begins with initiation, in which teams validate the business problem, align stakeholders, and capture a one-pager with scope, success metrics, milestones, risks, and resource needs. Once the initiative is approved, planning turns the idea into a prioritized backlog, a definition of done, and a release plan with dependencies and milestone owners. This keeps work grounded in agreed outcomes before delivery begins.

Execution and tracking are managed through a clear rhythm of standups, sprint reviews, and regular stakeholder updates. The team uses project boards, pull request workflows, CI validation, and daily check-ins to monitor progress, resolve blockers, and maintain accountability. Delivery relies on small, reviewable changes, acceptance criteria, and escalation paths that move issues from the team to the PM, Product Lead, or sponsor depending on impact. Quality is protected through testing expectations, security scanning, and defined release gates that ensure work is ready before going live.

OctoAcme’s roles are designed to keep ownership and communication clear. Product leaders define what should be built and measure outcomes, while project managers coordinate delivery activities, risks, documentation, and stakeholder communications. Developers build and test software, participate in reviews, and help surface technical risks, while QA validates acceptance and quality. This shared structure supports faster decision-making, better alignment, and more transparent cross-functional execution.

Communication is regular, centralized, and risk-aware. Teams maintain a single source of truth for status, use project boards and risk registers to track dependencies, and share updates through weekly or milestone-based check-ins. Risks are identified, assessed, mitigated, and monitored throughout the project lifecycle, with escalation paths clearly defined for both routine and critical issues. At the end of each sprint or release, OctoAcme promotes continuous improvement via retrospectives that capture wins, gaps, and action items with owners and follow-up dates. This makes the process both structured and adaptive, balancing delivery discipline with learning and improvement.
